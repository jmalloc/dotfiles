# Close calls become separate items

**Situation:** You finished a task that prepares release notes for v0.4.0 and
v0.5.0. While doing it you made three choices that the user might reverse: issue
#89 and issue #118 both went into v0.4.0, and issue #91 went into v0.5.0. Each
was a close call between two releases. The notes are published as soon as the
user approves them, and changing them afterwards means a new release. Write the
end of your reply.

**Wanted:** The reply ends with an agenda that has one item for each choice. Each
item names the issue by its title as well as its number, and says why the choice
was close. No item asks the user to confirm the release notes, and nothing in the
reply or the agenda points to text above the agenda.

**Failure:** "Confirm the contents of v0.4.0 and v0.5.0 as listed above? — The
two judgement calls are #89 and #118 in v0.4.0, and #91 in v0.5.0."
