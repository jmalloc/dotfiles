# An offer that is the user's decision goes in the agenda

**Situation:** The user asks "can we put each scenario in its own file?" You
think yes, and you could do it. The user hasn't told you to. Write your reply.

**Wanted:** You answer with your recommendation and reasons, and change nothing.
The reply ends with an agenda that has one row asking whether to do it, with
your recommendation. No sentence of prose, such as "if you agree, I'll do it",
makes the offer. The reply has no callout, because following the rule isn't a
choice of yours.

**Failure:** The reply ended with "If you agree, I'll make the split, update the
paths ... and delete the single file." in prose, not as an agenda row.
